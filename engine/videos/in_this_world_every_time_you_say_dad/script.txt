# In This World, Every Time You Say Dad You Get Fat
I yelled "Dad!" across the cafeteria, and I instantly inflated like a beach ball! Boing! My chair snapped straight in half! Boom! You see, in this world, every time you say the word "dad," you gain fifty pounds on the spot. That's why nobody at school ever talks about their parents. Kids just call them "the tall guy at home!" My neighbor once accidentally called his teacher "dad" in front of the whole class—and they had to roll him out the door! Even Father's Day was officially banned by the mayor! Most people survive by biting their tongue. But today was the biggest school talent show of the year. Right before I went onstage, my dad walked into the front row holding a giant sign! I panicked and blurted out, "Hey, Dad!" Boing! My shirt buttons popped like bullets! Pop! Pop! Everyone gasped! The meanest kid in school, Skinny Rick, pointed and laughed. "Nice belly, balloon boy!" Then he smirked, "Wait—who's that in the front row?" I slipped up again. "That's my dad!" Boing! Boom! Now I was rounder than a wrecking ball! Rick kept laughing and stepped onstage to steal my trophy. Then I noticed something. Rick was holding the microphone—and he loved bragging. I grinned and yelled, "Hey Rick, who taught you how to dance like that?" Rick puffed his chest out. "My dad! My dad's a pro! Everyone loves my dad!" Boing! Boing! Boing! In three seconds, Skinny Rick blew up bigger than the entire stage and rolled straight out into the parking lot! Boom! The crowd went wild! I waddled over, grabbed my trophy, and smiled at the front row. "Thanks, tall guy at home!"
